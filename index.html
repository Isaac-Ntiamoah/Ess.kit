Quick questions

Who is it for?

Anyone writing a scholarship, university or application essay.

I don't have a dramatic story. Is that a problem?

No. The kit says you don't need a dramatic story, you need a specific one. A small, real moment beats a big, fake one.

How long does it take?

The kit suggests a 10-minute timer per block, so you can finish a whole first draft in a weekend.

Will it work for my prompt and word limit?

Yes. The prompt matcher shows where to spend your words, and the word budget covers 250, 500 and 650-word essays.
